The pitch

What is it?

A group of unqualified contestants entered a cheap, badly organized televised sports festival by mistake. Every challenge starts as a normal, readable sport, then slowly falls apart into physics chaos that the players cause and understand. The comedy comes from play itself: timing, falls, and reactions, not written jokes.

The challenges

First two events
Event 01

The Flame That Refuses to Stay Lit

Carry an unstable flame through obstacles, wind, and water. Crossing first is not enough: whatever flame you have left counts toward your result.

  • Running fast weakens the flame; careful moves keep it alive
  • A short risky route and a longer safe one
  • Three checkpoints protect your progress
Event 02

Throw Anything

Pick sports gear or junk with different weights and values, then throw it at fixed and moving targets. Each player gets 60 seconds, and only the best three throws count.

  • Light, medium, and heavy objects
  • Aim, charge, release, and valid-landing checks
  • Fans and slippery floors bend your throws

Sabotage items

Mess makes it fun

Every tool gives a clear warning, a short effect, a way to counter it, and brief protection after a hit. No tool can ever steal points.

01Budget Breeze

A leaf blower that blasts players and light objects.

Brace, or step out of the blast line.

02Hydro Help

A water jet that soaks the target and leaves the floor slippery.

Dodge the spray or jump the puddle.

03Sports Delivery System

A shopping-cart cannon that fires sports balls down the track.

Watch the barrel and sidestep.

04Performance Enhancer

Sticks to a rival and vibrates, briefly shaking their aim.

Short resistance after the first hit.

05Motivation Horn

A loud blast that makes nearby players drop what they carry.

Stay out of range, or brace.

06Grip Improvement Mat

A sticky mat that pins anyone who steps on it for a moment.

You can see it, so jump over it.

07Equipment Stabilizer

A magnet that pulls metal gear and ruins throws.

Let go, or leave the field.

08Fair Play Shield

Blocks incoming stuff and sometimes bounces it back.

The one purely defensive tool.

The cast

Meet the contestants

The contestants

Chunky characters about three heads tall, with big hands and feet that read clearly in the middle of the chaos. Everyone shares one skeleton and one base body, with swappable headwear, top, bottom, shoes, and one accessory.

Cosmetics never change hitboxes, stats, or physics.

The Official

A second-hand robot hovering on two jets, clipboard in hand, running the tournament like a world-class event. It announces each challenge, explains the rules in under 20 seconds, and cheers every failure with enthusiasm nobody deserves.

Design pillars

The rules we play by
  • Understood in secondsEach briefing takes 20 seconds or less, then you play.
  • Readable chaosYou always know what hit you and who caused it.
  • Responsive controlThe comedy comes from consequences, not bad controls. No full-time floppy ragdoll.
  • Comebacks, not giftsTrailing players get more options, never a free win.
  • Built to reuseOne modular stadium, rebuilt for every event.
